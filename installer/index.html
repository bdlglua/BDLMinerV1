Solo Bitcoin mining on a pocket-sized ESP32

BDLMiner V1 is free, open-source firmware that turns an M5StickC Plus2 or another ESP32 board into a solo "lottery" Bitcoin miner, with a live screen, a web dashboard and automatic signed updates.

Install in 2 minutes GitHub Discord

Latest version: __VERSION__.

What it does

Everything runs on the board: it connects to a pool over WiFi, hashes block headers and shows what happens.

⚡

Hardware SHA-256

Uses the ESP32's SHA engine: about 1 MH/s on an M5StickC Plus2, with software mining as a fallback.

🔗

Stratum V1 and V2

Any solo pool, native Stratum V2 with Noise encryption, a backup pool and automatic failover.

📊

Live web dashboard

Hashrate, shares, what the pool sees, history, settings and logs from any browser on your network.

🔒

Signed automatic updates

New releases install by themselves, only with a valid signature, and roll back if they fail.

🔔

Alerts

ntfy, Discord or Telegram webhooks: best shares, low hashrate, a daily summary and, who knows, a block.

🏠

Home Assistant

MQTT discovery with controls, and Prometheus metrics for your own graphs.

🖥️

Made for small screens

Readable pages on every display from 0.9 to 2.8 inches, in English or French, or no screen at all.

🧪

ASIC boards too

Experimental support for the Bitaxe Gamma 601 and the NerdNOS, from hundreds of GH/s up to about 1 TH/s.

Supported boards

One firmware per family. Dashed boards are experimental: reports from real hardware are welcome.

    Install from your browser

    No software to install: Chrome or Edge on a computer flashes the board over USB.

    Your browser cannot install: use Chrome or Edge on a computer. The page must be opened over HTTPS.

    Version __VERSION__. The first install erases the board (settings and statistics).

    1. Plug the board into this computer with a USB cable that carries data.
    2. Pick the board, click Install and choose its serial port.
    3. When it restarts, join the WiFi network BDLMinerV1-XXXX (password MineYourCoins) and enter your WiFi, pool and BTC address.

    Then open the dashboard at http://bdlminer-xxxx.local. Updates arrive by themselves.

    A lottery ticket, not an income

    A block pays 3.125 BTC plus fees to your own address, but at 1 MH/s finding one takes billions of years on average. Every hash has the same tiny chance, with no progress between them: the fun is in the build, the statistics and chasing your best difficulty. Spend on it only what you would on a gadget.
