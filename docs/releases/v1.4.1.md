## Sharper physical display

- Use bolder native-size fonts for small labels to improve contrast and readability on compact LCDs.
- Fit numerical glyphs to their visible height, making hashrate, shares, difficulty and temperature readouts larger within the existing cards.
- Render common small illustrations directly at their display size to avoid a second resize and retain more detail.
- Keep the temperature readable on narrow displays, compacting the Celsius suffix or rounding to whole degrees when necessary instead of truncating the value.

Display assets remain in flash. Common illustration sizes require less runtime scaling, and headless firmware excludes the display assets. Mining kernels, requested power, thermal protection, saved settings and the local web dashboard retain their existing behavior. This release makes no new measured hashrate or temperature claims.

## Preview and validation

Previews use demonstration readings from the production renderer, not photographs of a physical miner. Physical LCD resolution still determines the available detail.

Validation covers seven display shapes in both languages, numerical glyph height, antialiased coverage, buffered/direct drawing parity, display caching and update recovery. Mining-core and release-tool regressions, 46 browser regressions, and builds and USB packaging for all ten board variants are retained.

## Updating

Choose the firmware for the same board as your current installation. Supported OTA updates preserve settings and statistics. Boards without OTA support use USB; a first installation with the web flasher erases the board. Signed firmware and the GitHub update manifest are included for all ten variants.
