# BDLMiner V2 2.4.1

Bitaxe Gamma 601 fix; settings, statistics, pool profiles and mining kernels are
otherwise unchanged from [2.4.0](v2.4.0.md).

- **Bitaxe Gamma 601: a silent ASIC is restarted automatically.** At 525 MHz, a
  BM1370 that stopped answering its hash-counter request for 3 s, or whose counter
  stalled, stopped mining with *ASIC not responding; restart required* until the
  board was restarted by hand. The chip is now reset and started again, as NerdNOS
  already does, up to **3 times an hour**; a fourth failure within the hour still
  stops mining until restart. The readings at each automatic restart (input and core
  voltage, current, temperatures, fan) are kept as the last fault, in the log and on
  the dashboard's ASIC tile. Above 525 MHz the existing one-time fallback to 525 MHz
  comes first. Regulator, sensor, fan and temperature faults still latch.
- The ASIC communication states now have French texts on the dashboard.

## Validation

Host regressions cover three automatic restarts in an hour, the latch on the fourth,
the hourly budget, and a failed shutdown that still latches, with all host, browser
and board builds. No physical board was used for this validation.
