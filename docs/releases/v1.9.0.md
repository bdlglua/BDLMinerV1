## The total hashrate of BDLMiner

How much do all the BDLMiner miners hash together? Starting with this version, each
miner can tell, anonymously, and the total is public on the
[BDLMiner statistics page](https://bdlminer-stats.bdlminer-bot.workers.dev/) and on the
[BDLMiner site](https://bdlglua.github.io/BDLMinerV1/#stats): total hashrate, number of
miners, best hour, a 7-day chart, and the split by board and by version.

- Once an hour, the first time 15 minutes after boot, the miner posts its **firmware
  version, its board and its hashrate** (the average of the last minute, rounded to 3
  digits), for instance `{"v":"1.9.0","board":"m5stickc-plus2","hashrate":1.02e6}`.
- **Nothing else is sent**: no Bitcoin address, worker, pool, name or identifier. The
  [statistics service](https://github.com/bdlglua/BDLMiner-stats) is open source; it keeps
  the reports 35 days, and the sender's network address only limits the number of
  reports, without being stored.
- A paused miner sends nothing, and the report waits when memory is short, like the
  other HTTPS requests.
- It is **on by default**. To turn it off: *Settings* → *Anonymous statistics* →
  **Share my hashrate anonymously**. It applies at once, without a restart. The daily
  [anonymous usage count](../../README.md#anonymous-usage-count) moved to the same
  section and is unchanged.

The [README](../../README.md#anonymous-hashrate-sharing) and the
[security policy](../../SECURITY.md) describe exactly what is sent.

## A site for BDLMiner

The web flasher at <https://bdlglua.github.io/BDLMinerV1/> is now the project's site,
at the same address: the screens, the features, the supported boards, the installer,
the live statistics, a word on the odds of finding a block, and the community links
(Discord, Reddit, GitHub), in English and French.

## Installation and compatibility

Existing BDLMiner devices install their matching signed **`*-firmware.bin`** through
GitHub update discovery (as a firmware trial) or the local firmware-update page;
settings, statistics and the hashrate history are kept. For a first install, pick the
board in the [web flasher](https://bdlglua.github.io/BDLMinerV1/) or flash its
**`*-factory.bin`** at 0x0. Signed firmware, signatures, factory images, SHA-256
checksums and the web flasher are provided for all **twelve variants**. The default
native user agent is **BDLMinerV1/1.9.0**.

## Validation

- Native host tests (new: the hashrate report's content and its schedule: first report,
  hourly cadence, paused miner, low memory, setting turned off), 75 browser regressions,
  the screen renderer on every display size, and the statistics service's own tests,
  also run in Cloudflare's runtime.
- The report itself had not reached the live service from a board before this release:
  the first reports will show on the statistics page within the hour after the update.
