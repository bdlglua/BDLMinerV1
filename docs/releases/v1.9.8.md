## The Bitaxe Gamma 601 mines steadily

1.9.7 brought the Gamma 601 to its first real start. Hours of mining on that board then
showed four more bugs; all are fixed. The Gamma is now verified mining on a real board.

### Bitaxe Gamma 601

- **No restart at each block.** Every new block made BDLMiner cut the ASIC's power and
  run its whole start-up again: about 10 seconds without hashing per block, and a power
  cycle every ten minutes. The ASIC now goes on with the new work, as in ESP-Miner.
- **No false overheat.** From power-on until the BM1370 routes its temperature diode,
  the sensor reads nonsense (106 °C on a 25 °C board): during one of those restarts,
  a 102 °C reading stopped the ASIC until restart. The diode is now ignored until it
  is routed (the board's sensor stands in), and a diode fault must hold for three
  readings in a row (0.75 s) before it stops the ASIC. A real overheat takes far
  longer: the 70 °C protection stays whole.
- **The last fault is kept.** The fault that last stopped the ASIC, with all its
  readings and its date, survives a restart: logged at boot and shown on the
  dashboard's ASIC tile.

### Bitaxe Gamma 601 and NerdNOS

- **Mining on any pool difficulty.** A pool that takes the miner for a NerdMiner (the
  `NerdMinerV2` user agent, for instance) drops its difficulty to 0.0032, and the ASIC
  was switched off below 16. As in ESP-Miner, it now keeps mining: its filter stays at
  16, so only those shares are sent. The odds of a block do not change; the pool shows
  a lower hashrate. Only a pool without version rolling still keeps the ASIC off.
- **A readable log.** An ASIC finds several 32-bit shares a second: a line each second
  kept only 1.5 minutes of log, and the beep or the *notify every 32-bit share* option
  would have fired as often. ASIC boards no longer log, beep or notify each one.

Nothing else changes since [1.9.7](v1.9.7.md).

## Installation and compatibility

Existing BDLMiner devices install their matching signed **`*-firmware.bin`** through
GitHub update discovery (as a firmware trial) or the local firmware-update page;
settings, statistics and the hashrate history are kept. For a first install, pick the
board in the [web flasher](https://bdlglua.github.io/BDLMinerV1/) or flash its
**`*-factory.bin`** at 0x0. Signed firmware, signatures, factory images, SHA-256
checksums and the web flasher are provided for all **twelve variants**. The default
native user agent is **BDLMinerV1/1.9.8**.

## Validation

- On a real Bitaxe Gamma 601 at the stock 525 MHz: about **1.07 TH/s**, core 1.145 V
  and 13 A, input 5.26 V, ASIC near 52 °C, regulator near 55 °C, fan near 8,600 rpm,
  hardware errors 0.04 to 0.1 %, over 1,200 accepted shares and none rejected in three
  hours. A new block arrived with no restart (shares on the new job 5 seconds later),
  and a start-up shows no false temperature. Overclock profiles above 525 MHz are still
  untested on a board.
- New host tests: a false 102 °C reading between two normal ones, a diode that reads
  106 °C before it is routed, a real overheat confirmed and its fault kept across a
  restart, the ASIC kept running across a new block. All native host tests,
  75 browser regressions and the twelve builds pass.
