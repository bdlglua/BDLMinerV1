# BDLMiner V2 2.3.2

## Fix

- **Bitaxe Gamma 601: no more ASIC restarts after HTTPS connections.** A TLS
  handshake (update check, market data, pool statistics, notifications) can hold
  the main loop for 2-2.5 seconds (`[loop] stalled 2455 ms` in the log). Mining
  permission, renewed by that loop once a second and valid for 3 seconds, then
  expired: the Gamma powered its ASIC off and repeated its 9-second start
  (*Initializing BM1370*). The loop now renews it on every pass and it lasts
  10 seconds. The thermal policy still decides the power every second, and the
  ASIC drivers keep their own hard limits, checked every 250 ms.

Settings, statistics, mining kernels and the 2.3.1 changes are unchanged.

## Validation

Host regressions cover the permission across a 2.5-second stall just before the
policy tick, pauses kept as pauses, expiry of a stopped loop and timer wrap, with
all host, browser and board builds. No physical board was used for this validation.
