## The local dashboard in the site's style

The miner's own web pages (dashboard, settings, log) now look like the
[BDLMiner site](https://bdlglua.github.io/BDLMinerV1/):

- a sticky **top bar** instead of the side menu: the chip logo and **BDLMiner V1**, the
  three pages with an orange line under the current one, and the status badge with a
  glowing dot (green online, red disconnected);
- the site's colours, panels, buttons (orange, or outlined for secondary actions) and
  text size, and the selected chart period in orange;
- on phones the bar keeps only the logo's chip so that the three pages fit, and on the
  smallest screens the status badge keeps its dot.

Everything works as before: the same pages, tiles, charts, settings and log.

## Fixes

- **Log**: a line without a time or a tag (very early at boot) was squeezed into the
  time column, one word per line; it now takes the whole width.
- **Hashrate sharing**: 1.9.4 sent the model of an M5StickC Plus2 although it only
  repeats the board's name ("M5StickC Plus2" and "M5StickC-Plus2" differ by a dash), so
  the statistics showed it on a line of its own. Names are now compared without case,
  spaces and punctuation; the statistics service already ignores such a model.

Nothing else changes since [1.9.4](v1.9.4.md).

## Installation and compatibility

Existing BDLMiner devices install their matching signed **`*-firmware.bin`** through
GitHub update discovery (as a firmware trial) or the local firmware-update page;
settings, statistics and the hashrate history are kept. For a first install, pick the
board in the [web flasher](https://bdlglua.github.io/BDLMinerV1/) or flash its
**`*-factory.bin`** at 0x0. Signed firmware, signatures, factory images, SHA-256
checksums and the web flasher are provided for all **twelve variants**. The default
native user agent is **BDLMinerV1/1.9.5**. The pages take no more flash than before
(the M5Stack Core build stays at 92.6 %).
