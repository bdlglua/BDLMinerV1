# BDLMiner V2 2.5.3

Bitaxe Gamma 601 overclocking and a new web interface; mining on the other boards
is unchanged from [2.5.2](v2.5.2.md) and the update installs over WiFi.

- **Profiles up to 800 MHz.** Local Settings > Safety offers 525 to 800 MHz in 25 MHz
  steps, and ESP-Miner's 690 MHz preset (up to 2.5.2: 690 MHz at most). Above 690 MHz
  is the overclocking range AxeOS leaves to custom values.
- **The voltage that goes with it.** A new 1300 mV core voltage, and choosing a profile
  raises the voltage to its own: 1150 mV up to 600 MHz, 1200 mV up to 675, 1250 mV up
  to 725, 1300 mV up to 800. It is never lowered automatically and can still be chosen
  by hand.
- **Temperature limits as in ESP-Miner.** The ASIC stops at 75 °C (was 70 °C) and the
  regulator at 105 °C (was 90 °C), ESP-Miner's own values. The thermal limit of the
  settings goes up to 73 °C (was 68 °C) so that mining still pauses before that stop.
  The current (30 A), fan, supply and voltage-window stops are unchanged, and so is
  the fallback to 525 MHz on instability.
- **A new web interface.** The miner's dashboard, settings and log pages, the
  Linux/Raspberry Pi hub and the website share one design in the style of
  exchange templates: flat dark surfaces, solid blue actions, green change badges. The website is now built with
  React and Next.js; its home page shows the live fleet by board, with
  family tabs and a search. It opens dark, with
  a button for a light theme. Pages, settings and controls are where they were.

Overclocking means more heat and power: watch the ASIC and regulator temperatures, with
a heatsink and the 5 V fan properly mounted. Profiles above 625 MHz have not been
validated on a physical Gamma 601.

## Validation

All host and browser tests pass, with new checks of the 800 MHz profile at 1300 mV
(PLL and regulator command), of the 75 °C and 105 °C stops (and nothing below them) and
of the voltage that the settings page pairs with each profile. Not yet run on a Gamma.
