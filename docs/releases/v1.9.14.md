## Durable block candidates, chain observations and clearer displays

Owner-run LAN reports cover M5StickC Plus2
and Gamma 601 OTA confirmation to 1.9.14, controlled Gamma rollback, and the
1.9.13 dashboard comparison. The validation record below distinguishes these
observations from offline tests and the remaining hardware checks.

### Block candidates and saved evidence

- Give network-target candidates reserved priority storage so an ordinary-share
  queue cannot silently displace them. Deduplicate recent candidate hashes and
  exclude obsolete work from discovery counts and alerts.
- Retain the proof's original height and full hash. Announce discoveries already
  present before the first watch tick; update the lifetime count immediately.
- Persist the latest candidate hash, height and submission state in alternating
  CRC-checked NVS extension slots, with verified writes, retry and corrupt-slot
  fallback. Existing statistics formats remain readable by rollback firmware.
- Show restored proofs as belonging to a previous session. Never replay them or
  repeat the discovery alert; interrupted queued/submitted work becomes stale
  or unanswered, respectively. Candidate evidence remains device-local NVS.
- Track submitted/accepted/rejected/unanswered states independently of the
  ordinary share log. Use **Block candidate** wording: a share ACK is not a
  Bitcoin block confirmation or a promise of payment.

### Explorer observations

- Check a candidate's hash through mempool.space over certificate/hostname
  verified HTTPS, preserving the mining-safe TLS/watchdog/heap handling.
- Validate reported height and tip, display the source and confirmation count,
  and keep the pool's payout status separate. This is an explorer observation,
  not independent full-chain verification by the ESP32.
- Recheck reorganizations. HTTP/certificate errors, malformed or oversized
  responses and unindexed hashes keep current chain acceptance uncertain.
- Make no explorer request without a candidate. Delay the first check to let the
  pool submit, then refresh with bounded polling/backoff. Late results cannot
  attach to a newer candidate. Observations are refreshed after reboot.
- Reject negative, zero and overflowing compact network targets in Stratum V1/V2.

### Physical display and previews

- Keep Gamma OLED rendering at its actual **128×32 monochrome** resolution,
  explicitly select the 6×8 font and use four bounded rows with side margins.
- Compact accepted/rejected counters so both remain visible, shorten voltage
  labels, show missing readings as `--`, and center the candidate alert.
- Page complete long fault/setup text instead of silently truncating it;
  preserve UTF-8 boundaries, reset paging on change and isolate control characters.
- Add native Gamma renderer checks separate from the seven color-screen formats.
  Export color overview sheets with exact integer zoom instead of extra smoothing.

### Validation and release preparation

- Add a full loopback TCP Stratum test: real work/header generation, ASIC proof
  verification, priority submission, independently reconstructed genesis hash,
  pool acceptance/rejection, disconnect, clean work before ACK and saved outcome
  after reboot. No proof is sent to a public pool.
- Add a portable **read-only** LAN tool for real statistics, dashboard closed/open
  measurements and observation of OTA trial confirmation/rollback. Counter
  resets and missing trial evidence cannot count as successful measurements.
- Owner-run OTA checks: M5 confirmed on 1.9.14 at approximately 1.005 MH/s and
  54 C; Gamma returned to 1.9.13 during its controlled trial-reset test, then
  confirmed 1.9.14 at approximately 1.066 TH/s, 525 MHz, ASIC 54 C, regulator
  58.75 C and automatic fan 79% at 8,667 RPM. The Gamma accepted 14 shares with
  no rejects in the final session and reported no regulator fault or fallback.
- Owner-run dashboard comparison under 1.9.13: M5 -0.563%, Gamma +0.100%, with
  unchanged maximum temperatures in approximately three-minute phases. This
  does not establish prolonged stability or dashboard performance on all boards.
- The owner confirmed both M5 and Gamma physical screens are readable on 1.9.14.
- Controlled fan/thermal response and M5 rollback remain unverified. No NerdNOS
  device was available for these hardware checks. LAN observations came from
  owner-run reports; the cloud workspace could not reach the boards directly.
- Provide draft preparation through `release/prepare-X.Y.Z`, including signed
  firmware and an installer preview, while leaving published updates unchanged.
- Keep all **12 board variants**, existing user settings/manual identities,
  SHA-256d kernels, CPU/ASIC clocks and hard thermal/fan/supply protections.
