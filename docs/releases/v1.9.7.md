## The Bitaxe Gamma 601 on a real board

BDLMiner's Gamma 601 version had never run on a real board. On the first one, it stopped
at its regulator's setup: no Gamma could have started. An audit against ESP-Miner and
the TPS546D24A datasheet, then that board, found these bugs; all are fixed.

### The regulator

- **Its relative mode is accepted.** The TPS546 keeps its voltage limits relative to the
  output voltage by default, a mode BDLMiner refused ("unsupported VOUT_MODE"). In
  absolute mode, the limits are now written in volts.
- **Its own formats and steps are accepted.** Each setting is read back and compared as
  a value: the regulator may store a number with another exponent, and rounds current
  limits to 2 A and voltage limits to steps of 1 to 10%. Temperatures and timings are
  written as ESP-Miner does. A setting that does not hold is named in the log.
- **Only real faults stop mining.** As in ESP-Miner: power off, overvoltage, overcurrent,
  input undervoltage and temperature. The other status bits also report warnings, and
  one stays latched; they no longer stop the ASIC. The measured limits still apply.

### Start-up

- **Stock 525 MHz by default**, as ESP-Miner, instead of an experimental 600 MHz. A
  profile saved earlier is kept; 550 to 800 MHz stay in *Settings*.
- **It waits for the 5 V supply.** On USB alone, the regulator does not answer and the
  fan does not turn: the setup is tried again every 5 seconds, the dashboard says
  *plug in the 5V supply*, and mining starts once the barrel jack is in.
- **A sagging supply no longer stops it for good:** 4.8 V to start, then down to 4.6 V
  while mining (the regulator itself stops at 4.5 V).

### Temperature

- While the ASIC is off, its diode gives no reading. Mining was then limited to 25% at
  boot, and after a first pause it would have stayed paused for good. The board's own
  sensor now takes over, on the dashboard and on the OLED screen, which showed
  "-- C"; `temperature_source` says which one (`asic` or `board`).

### Every board

- **The WiFi setup portal pauses between two passes.** Its loop never did; the Gamma
  restarted twice on the task watchdog during its setup.

Every fault on the Gamma also logs all its readings (regulator status, voltages,
current, temperatures, fan). Nothing else changes since [1.9.6](v1.9.6.md).

## Installation and compatibility

Existing BDLMiner devices install their matching signed **`*-firmware.bin`** through
GitHub update discovery (as a firmware trial) or the local firmware-update page;
settings, statistics and the hashrate history are kept. For a first install, pick the
board in the [web flasher](https://bdlglua.github.io/BDLMinerV1/) or flash its
**`*-factory.bin`** at 0x0. Signed firmware, signatures, factory images, SHA-256
checksums and the web flasher are provided for all **twelve variants**. The default
native user agent is **BDLMinerV1/1.9.7**. The Gamma needs a pool that accepts version
rolling and a difficulty of at least 16, such as `public-pool.io:3333` with a suggested
difficulty of 1024.

## Validation

- On a real Bitaxe Gamma 601: "regulator and sensors configured", 5.41 V input, fan
  8,837 rpm, regulator and board at 24 °C, regulator held off. **The BM1370 has not been
  powered yet**: its first start, real hashrate and accepted shares remain to be
  verified.
- New host tests: a regulator that stores numbers in its own format and rounds them,
  relative and absolute modes, warnings that no longer stop mining, waiting for the
  supply and the fan, the board temperature while the ASIC is off. All native host
  tests, 75 browser regressions and the twelve builds pass.
