## Hashrate history kept, daily summary, recent shares

**Hashrate history kept across restarts.** The 24-hour chart used to start over at
every restart, update or power cut, and the hashrate alert lost its reference for
half an hour. The history is now saved every 30 minutes and before each restart, in a
flash area that settings never use (rotating over 64 sectors, a save cut short leaves
the previous one valid), and restored once the clock is set; the time the miner was
off appears as a gap in the chart.

**No false alarm after a power cut during an update trial.** Any restart during the
3-minute trial of a new firmware brings the previous one back. One caused by a power
cut, the reset button or a USB serial connection no longer counts as a failure: no
"Update rolled back" notification, and the version is installed again (three such
interruptions in a row do count).

**Daily summary.** At the hour of your choice (20:00 by default, *Off* to stop it), a
notification gives the day's average hashrate, best share and record, accepted and
rejected shares and the time connected to the pool.

**Recent shares.** The dashboard lists the last 20 shares sent, with the time, their
difficulty and the pool's answer (accepted, rejected, waiting, or no answer when the
connection dropped first).

**Why a pool lists no worker.** Public-pool now imposes a minimum difficulty of 1 and
lists only workers with a share in the last 10 minutes: at about 1 MH/s, one share
comes every 70 minutes. The *Seen by the pool* tile now says so, with the interval,
instead of an unexplained "0 workers".

## Installation and compatibility

Existing BDLMiner devices install the matching signed **`*-firmware.bin`** through
GitHub update discovery (as a firmware trial) or the local firmware-update page. For
M5StickC Plus2, use **`m5stickc-plus2-firmware.bin`**. Settings and saved statistics
are kept; the hashrate history starts being saved from this version on. Signed
firmware, signatures, factory images, SHA-256 checksums and the web flasher are
provided for all **eleven variants**. The default native user agent is
**BDLMinerV1/1.7.0**.

## Validation

- 74 browser regressions, native host tests (share log included) and eleven board
  builds.
- On an M5StickC Plus2: the history saved after 30 minutes came back after a restart
  ("5 points, 0 while off"); a reset during the trial of a GitHub update was
  recognised as an interruption, the version installed again and confirmed.
